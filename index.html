At CNR since 2019, now at ICCOM in Pisa. I don't do research myself: I make other people's research possible, with lab, data, design and coding skills.

What I love about this job? It's versatile and dynamic: every day is different and you never quite know what to expect. I'm at home where different trades overlap, and when a tool I need doesn't exist, I try to build it. How I got here is further down, in The story so far.

Something about me: I'm deaf and wear a cochlear implant 🦻. It has never held me back, it just taught me to adapt. That's why email is always the best way to reach me.

Dai file di testo degli strumenti a grafici puliti, pronti da consultare e da usare nelle pubblicazioni.

From instrument text files to clean plots, ready to browse and to use in publications.

problemaproblem
Ogni strumento esporta i dati nel suo formato, e rifare i grafici a mano, uno per uno, porta via tempo.Every instrument exports data in its own format, and re-plotting each one by hand eats up time.
soluzionesolution
Carichi un file o un'intera cartella (FTIR, Raman, UV-Vis, fluorescenza, HPLC e altri): riconosce lo strumento, trova i picchi e ne suggerisce i nomi, che inserisce solo se li approvi. Poi puoi confrontare, sovrapporre e ritoccare.You drop in a file or a whole folder (FTIR, Raman, UV-Vis, fluorescence, HPLC and more): it detects the instrument, finds the peaks and suggests their names, adding them only if you approve. Then you can compare, overlay and fine-tune.
risultatoresult
In pochi secondi un PDF da sfogliare, immagini PNG a 600 dpi e SVG pronte per tesi e articoli, l'elenco dei picchi e un Excel per Origin. Senza installare nulla: i dati non lasciano il computer.In seconds: a browsable PDF, 600 dpi PNG and SVG images ready for theses and papers, a peak list and an Excel file for Origin. Nothing to install: data never leaves your computer.

Sample IDs include the operator's initials, to avoid duplicates across PCs.
